About ALTIS SALES

An independent sourcing partner

ALTIS SALES is an independent industrial sourcing company based in France. We are not a manufacturer and we are not exclusively tied to any factory.

For each request, we select manufacturing partners according to your technical specifications, production quantities, commercial objectives and delivery expectations, and we coordinate the project on your behalf.

We work primarily with industrial companies across Europe, and support international projects.

We work with

  • Procurement managers and purchasing directors
  • Engineering and R&D departments
  • Mold makers and plastics processors
  • Machine builders and industrial manufacturers
  • Maintenance teams looking for spare parts

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are you a manufacturer?

No. ALTIS SALES is an independent sourcing company. We identify suitable manufacturers for your parts and coordinate the project with them on your behalf.

Which files can I send?

Whatever you have: PDF or DWG/DXF drawings, 3D models (STEP, IGES, STL, SolidWorks…), photos, catalog references, or a physical sample. Large files can be shared through a transfer link.

Will my drawings stay confidential?

Yes. Your files are only shared with the manufacturers consulted for your part, and we can sign a non-disclosure agreement before you send anything.

What quantities do you handle?

From prototypes to production runs. Tell us your volumes and we will look for manufacturers suited to them.

How do you manage quality?

Manufacturers are checked before any order, the first article or sample is approved by you, and each shipment can be inspected before it leaves the factory.

Which countries do you serve?

We work mainly with companies across Europe, and we support international projects on request.
