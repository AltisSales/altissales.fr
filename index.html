FAQ

Frequently asked questions

I'm a supplier. Can you help me find new projects?

Yes. We help suppliers identify qualified project owners, develop business opportunities and build long-term partnerships across Europe and beyond.

What do you need from me?

Your company presentation: processes, machines, certifications and references.

Who do I sign the contract with?

The contract is signed directly between the project owner and the supplier. ALTIS SALES prepares the ground: we clarify the requirements, align both sides on price, quality and lead times, and stay involved until you agree.

How do you choose the suppliers?

We assess suppliers based on production capabilities, equipment, quality standards, references and project requirements.

Will my drawings stay confidential?

Yes. Shared only with the suppliers we introduce, under NDA if you wish.

Which files can I send?

PDF, DWG/DXF, STEP, IGES, STL, SolidWorks, photos or a sample. Large files via a transfer link.
