About ALTIS SALES

An independent business introducer

Based in France, tied to no manufacturer: for each request we suggest those that best fit the specification, quantities, budget and lead time.

We never buy, resell or invoice parts. Price, quality and delivery are agreed directly between buyer and manufacturer.

We work with

  • Procurement and purchasing
  • Engineering and R&D
  • Mold makers and plastics processors
  • Machine builders
  • Manufacturers seeking European customers

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are you a manufacturer or a reseller?

Neither. We find, introduce and mediate. We don't make, buy or resell parts.

Who do I sign the contract with?

The manufacturer. Quotation, order, invoice and delivery are between you.

How are you paid?

A commission agreed in advance with the manufacturer, only on business we introduced. No fee for buyers.

How do you choose the manufacturers?

By part, quantity and requirements. Each one is checked first: real production, equipment, references, export experience.

Will my drawings stay confidential?

Yes. Shared only with the manufacturers we introduce, under NDA if you wish.

Which files can I send?

PDF, DWG/DXF, STEP, IGES, STL, SolidWorks, photos or a sample. Large files via a transfer link.

I'm a manufacturer: how do we work together?

Present your capabilities with "I'm a manufacturer". If they match our buyers' needs, we agree on a commission before any introduction.
